How to ethically generate text:

When generating text using AI, NEVER ask AI to write the entire thing for you.

No matter how big or small the text is, you should never ask AI to generate an entire piece of text for you.

If you do, you won't actually understand the text you're generating, and the people who are reading the text will most likely use an AI detector on it.

Some examples of bad prompts for text are:

  • "Generate this essay for me."
  • "Write a paper about plant growth."
  • "Can you generate an entire article about WW2 battle tanks for me?"

While there are certainly wrong ways to ask for writing help via AI, there are also some good ways.

When asking help from AI, try asking it some questions that can certainly help you get your footing, while also ensuring that you are the only one writing the text.

Using this method can help you understand your material a lot more, and it will be obvious to the readers that you are the one who wrote the text.

Some examples of good prompts for text are:

  • Can you help explain and showcase some points for me to use on this essay?
  • How can I start and transition between paragraphs in my paper about plant growth to ensure smoothness throughout the text?
  • Can you give me some details about famous WW2 battle tanks that I could write about in an article?

These prompts are a lot better than the former ones because they help give the writer some points to use on their text, while also making sure that the writer is actually the one writing.

In conclusion, when asking AI for help when writing a text, you should never ask it to write the text completely.

Instead, you should ask the AI questions to help you get your footing on an otherwise confusing writing prompt, so you can write a quality text while also ensuring that you are the only one who wrote it.
